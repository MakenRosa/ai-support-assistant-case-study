# Technical note: RAG and content updates

## Scope

This note discusses common options. It does not disclose a company's model strategy or internal decision.

## Consideration

Retrieval can provide context from a knowledge collection at response time. Updating that collection and changing model behavior through training are different activities.

## Tradeoff

Retrieval requires suitable content, retrieval quality and response evaluation. Fine-tuning also requires its own data, objectives and evaluation. The choice depends on the task; neither option guarantees an accurate response.

No actual corpus, provider, model, cost, training material or internal benchmark is included.
