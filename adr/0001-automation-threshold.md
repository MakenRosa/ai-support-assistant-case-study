# Technical note: human review

## Scope

This is a general discussion, not an internal policy or decision record.

## Consideration

Applications using generated responses need to decide where human review is appropriate. Model output may be incomplete or incorrect, and a confidence indication alone does not establish correctness.

## Tradeoff

Human review can add time and effort while providing a way to handle ambiguity. Appropriate criteria depend on the use case and require evaluation.

No company's thresholds, escalation rules, automation rates or operating practices are published here.
